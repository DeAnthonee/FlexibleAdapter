      How to play

      • Goal: reach 20 ★ or be the last monster alive. Everyone starts with 10 ♥.
      • Your turn: roll 6 dice, reroll any of them up to 2 more times, then resolve.
      • 1 / 2 / 3: three of a number scores that many ★. Each extra matching die adds 1 ★.
      • ⚡ Energy: each one gives you a cube. Spend them on cards.
      • ♥ Heart: heal 1 each. You cannot heal with dice while in Tokyo. Max 10 ♥.
      • 🐾 Claw: 1 damage each. Inside Tokyo you hit everyone outside. Outside Tokyo you hit whoever is inside.
      • Tokyo: if it is empty, the first monster to roll a claw moves in. +1 ★ when you enter, +2 ★ at the start of each turn you stay. When hit, you may yield and the attacker takes your place.
      • Cards: after rolling, buy any of the 3 face-up cards with ⚡, or pay 2 ⚡ to sweep them. Keep cards stay with you. Discard cards fire once.
      • Tokyo Bay: with 5 or 6 players a second monster can occupy Tokyo. Both count as "in Tokyo".
